Built for long range: the shot's vapour trail is read for crosswind and turned into a scope correction, then the group is scored to confirm it. Centrefire rifle in .308, 6.5 Creedmoor, .223 and similar chamberings out to 1000 yards, with rimfire and air disciplines supported at shorter ranges.
About the developer
BAS is developed by Dr Artur Krukowski. It integrates target scoring and vapour-trail ballistics into one application, sharing a design principle: the physics and the rules are written down explicitly in the source, with their assumptions stated, so a shooter can see exactly why the app produced the number it did.
BAS is a training and practice aid. Official match results are determined by the appointed jury under the current rulebook of the governing body. Ring dimensions and rule parameters shipped with the app are nominal published figures and are editable — verify them against the rulebook in force for your competition.
Operate the app only from behind the firing line, with the firearm made safe and the range in a condition permitted by the range officer. Never handle a phone and a loaded firearm at the same time.

Lock exposure and focus
A phone camera re-meters and re-focuses continuously. Between two frames of the same card that changes the brightness of everything, which is precisely what differential detection looks for, and re-focusing shifts the field of view enough to make the registration stale. Locking happens automatically when you register; this releases it if the light changes for real.
